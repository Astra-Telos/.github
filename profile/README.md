# Astra Telos

**Toward AGI.**

Astra Telos is a technology organization focused on researching and developing **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)**.

---

## Introduction

Astra Telos pursues a long-term goal: to build an intelligence system capable of learning, reasoning, adapting, and solving problems across a broad scope — not limited to a single task or domain.

We believe AGI will be the next turning point for humanity. Therefore, Astra Telos devotes its full resources to foundational research, methods, and systems aimed at AGI.
